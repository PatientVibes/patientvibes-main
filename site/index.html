Missouri school districts & city limits

How to use

Search for a city, or click any city or district on the map. The list shows which school districts overlap the city and roughly how much of the city each covers.

District lines: Missouri DESE. Data downloaded recently. City limits: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb incorporated places (legal boundaries as of the Census vintage, which can lag recent annexations). Confirm any address with the district before enrolling.
